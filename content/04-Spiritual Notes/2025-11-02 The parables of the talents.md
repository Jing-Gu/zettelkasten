Also known as the parable of the bags of gold

>“Again, it will be like a man going on a journey, who called his servants and entrusted his wealth to them.( Matthew 25:14 NIV )

Man going on a journey: Jesus
Servants: people who profess to know and serve Christ

> Every good and perfect gift is from above. ( James 1:17)

Such as life, breath, salvation, money, spiritual gifts, time, trial

The question is not "what have you been given",  but "What are you doing with what you've been given"?

The ending of the third servant was being casted out, or hell, as he didn't maximize the return for his master's gold, that was the foolish act, he didn't really know Christ, because he didn't do anything except for burying the gold. 

There was a slide about two questions, on the left side, how to get saved, and on the right side, how to get lost. Apparently, believe, so you get saved, however, on the right side the answer was empty, and that's exactly the thing, there's no action to be done to be lost, simply doing nothing will lead to the lost.
