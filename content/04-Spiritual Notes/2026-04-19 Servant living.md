Servants know God's grace

>As God’s co-workers we urge you not to receive God’s grace in vain. ( 2 Corinthians 6:1 NIV )

  
Servants experience extra difficulties 

Servants display the fruit of godly characters 

Servants live in the tension of spiritual paradox 

The message below describes exactly the paradox:

>through glory and dishonor, bad report and good report; genuine, yet regarded as impostors; known, yet regarded as unknown; dying, and yet we live on; beaten, and yet not killed; sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; poor, yet making many rich; having nothing, and yet possessing everything. ( 2 Corinthians 6:8-10 NIV )

I remembered the pastor's analogy to express this paradox where joy and sorrow are often at the same time. People often hear the analogy of mountain (good time) and valley (sorrow). However, another fresh view is that life is a railway track touching both sides together, that explains sorrowful yet always rejoicing.

Servants are intent on loving others
