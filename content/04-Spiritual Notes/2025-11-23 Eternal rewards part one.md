> For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each of us may receive what is due us for the things done while in the body, whether good or bad. 
> ( 2 Corinthians 5:10 NIV )

This judgement of reward is for believers, different from the white throne judgement for non-believers mentioned in revelation.

Will God punish us for our sin at the judgement seat of Christ?
No. Blood of Jesus cleansed the sin for us.

Rewards come from many aspects:

Loving others, even enemies.

Speak in love, act in love, think in love.

Although love doesn't mean tolerate everything from others.

Jesus,  in his love he speaks the truth, and shows the grace.

  

How we handle the money

"For where you treasure is, there your heart will be also."

Money given for the god purpose is the eternal investment that has eternal value or reward.
