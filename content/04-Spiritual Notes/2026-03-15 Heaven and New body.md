God has promised to give us a perfect glorified body


We are **body, soul, spirit**.

Soul is true you, your emotions and wills, your consciousness.

Spirit is the consciousness of God. Animals don't have spirit.

  
God has given us his spirit as a guarantee of our glorified body 

God brings us, upon death, to be with him in heaven. 

We walk by faith, not by sight.

>For we live by faith, not by sight. ( 2 Corinthians 5:7 NIV )
