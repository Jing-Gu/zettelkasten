**Signs of second coming**

1. Spiritual deception 
	"For many will come in my name" 

2. Wars and rumors of wars
	"Nation will rise against nation"

3. Natural disasters 
	"Famine, diseases.... All those are the beginning of birth pains."

4. Persecution 
	"You will be hated by all nations because of me."

5. Apostasy 
	"The love of most will grow cold, but the one who stands firm to the end will be saved."

  
6. Worldwide gospel proclamation 
	The gospel will be preached in the whole world as a testimony to all nations, and then the end will come.
