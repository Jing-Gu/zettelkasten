The steps of church discipline 

According to Matthew 18:15-17

- Step 1: go privately to them

- Step 2: take a witness 

- Step 3: tell the church leaders

- Step 4: ask them to leave

The goal of church discipline: to restore them

**Forgiveness is a choice, not a feeling**

> Now instead, **you ought to forgive** and comfort him, so that he will not be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow. ( 2 Corinthians 2:7 NIV )

  
  Forgiveness is just between you and God. Reconciliation is between you, the other person and the God.

  Forgiveness can happen if one side is willing, reconciliation can only happen if two sides are willing.
