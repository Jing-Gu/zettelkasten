Who wrote it ?

Human author: Apostle Paul 
Divine author: All Scriptures are breathed by God

Why wrote it?

To lovely rebuke and correct the church, what to believe and how to act as Christians.

In front of sufferings, we normally have the **Victim** perspective: why did this happen to me, God must be against me.
But God wants us to have a **Victor** perspective: What can I learn from this, God will never leave me.

## Suffering draws us into a deeper relationship with the lord as we receive his comfort.

## Suffering prepares us to better help others.
>who comforts us in all our troubles, so that we can comfort those in any trouble with the comfort we ourselves receive from God. ( 2 Corinthians 1:4 NIV )

"God never wastes your pain." Frank Peretti 
## Suffering enables us to depend on God more fully 
> Indeed, we felt we had received the sentence of death. But this happened that we might not rely on ourselves but on God, who raises the dead. ( 2 Corinthians 1:9 NIV )

## Suffering produces a Christ-centred hope 
> He has delivered us from such a deadly peril, and he will deliver us again. On him we have set our hope that he will continue to deliver us, ( 2 Corinthians 1:10 NIV )
## Suffering is not meaningless or random, it has specific purpose for God bigger reason.

God will use your prayers for others, and your prayer might help others, never stop prayers nor stop asking others for prayers. 

Prayer is not the last resource, but should be the first resource.
