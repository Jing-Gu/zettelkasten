Do not be yoked together with unbelievers.

Analogy nowadays is not marrying to unbelievers. 

An idol is everything that you love more, fear more, value more and serve more than God.

> One thing I ask from the LORD, 
> this only do I seek: 
> that I may dwell in the house of the LORD 
> all the days of my life. 
> ( Psalm 27:4 NIV )
