Context: Paul was accused to be a fake teacher.

  

If you look at the world, you will be distressed.

If you look within, you will be depressed.

If you look at the god, you will be at peace.

  

Lens of our problems or lens of God's promises 

**Living by faith** means we take God promises and choose to believe them. God will never disappoint you.
