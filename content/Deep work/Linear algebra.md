Vectors
seen differently in physics, CS and math

**Linear combination** of two vectors $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{w}$.
When scaling two vectors and adding them, it is the linear combination. $a$ and $b$ are scalars.
$$a\vec{v} + b\vec{w}$$
The set of all possible vectors that you can reach with a linear combination of a given pair of vectors, is called the **span** of those two vectors. That's to say, let $a$ and $b$ vary over all real numbers, then we get the span of  $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{w}$.

Matrices are **linear transformation**.
Linear transformations are functions with vectors as inputs and vectors as outputs
