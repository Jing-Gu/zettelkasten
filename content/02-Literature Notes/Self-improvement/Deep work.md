# Summary & Reflection
When we switch from task A to task B, the brain cannot switch the context immediately, in fact it will linger on the previous context, which is called "the attention residue". Because of this, the shallow work is dangerous, frequent email checking will introduce attention residue all the time that breaks the long deep work.

Cut off the social media, be uneasy to reach. 

Deep work is meaningful, deep work leads to deep life, as we leave behind the distraction, the depth will grant us with the productivity and meaning.
