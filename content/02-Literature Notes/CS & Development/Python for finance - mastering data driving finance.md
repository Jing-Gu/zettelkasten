# Summary & Reflection

Python advantage: data analysis, visualization, machine learning packages

Finance evolution: Data-driven finance
2 factors
1. Programmatic access to almost all financial data
	Trading or investment decisions used to be decided by portfolio managers who read through newspapers or learn from conversations. Now most of today’s global equities trading volume is driven by **algorithms** and computers rather than by human traders.
2. Increasing AI in finance
	Capitalize on ML and DL algorithms to replace traditional financial theory in order to increase investment performance


important financial theories: MPT (modern financial theory), CAPM (capital asset pricing model)
