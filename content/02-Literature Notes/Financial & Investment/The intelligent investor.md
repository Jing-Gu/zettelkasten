# Summary & Reflection

### Investment vs speculation
What does it mean by being "intelligent"?

This kind of intelligence has nothing to do with IQ, it simply means being patient, disciplined and eager to learn, in fact it is a trait of character than of the brain.

According to Graham, the definition of investing should promise the **safety of principle** and an **adequate return**, not extraordinary.

### The investor and inflation
Inflation is so easy to overlook, so it's important to measure the investment success not just by what you make, but how much you make after inflation.

REITs is a inflation-fighter, why?
- Rents tend to rise with inflation. During inflation, landlords can often **increase rents**, especially in residential and commercial leases with short durations
- Property values tend to go up due to the **rising replacement costs** (materials, labor, construction).

### The investor and market fluctuations
Do not be the slave of Mr.Market, if your investment horizon is long, at least 25 or 30 years, then there is only one sensible approach: Buy every month, automatically, and whenever you can spare some money. The single best choice for the lifelong holding is a total stock-market index fund, and sell only when you need cash. 

### The investor and his advisors

Despite of different types of financial advisors, the question is do you need help? If yes, trust then verify, but the reality is no real good advisor will take you if you have less than $100,000 to invest.

Who is your enemy? Your advisor should protect you from your worst enemy - yourself. You hire an advisor not to manage your money but to manage you.
