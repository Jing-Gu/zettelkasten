# The Book in 3 Sentences

The investment philosophy comes before investment strategies. Once you have an investment philosophy in place, you develop investment strategies that build on the core philosophy. The same philosophy can spawn multiple strategies.

# Top Quotes

An investment philosophy is **a coherent way of thinking about markets**, how they work (and sometimes do not), and the types of mistakes that you believe consistently underlie investor behaviour.
# Summary & Reflection

Key elements to come up with an investment philosophy:
- Understand risk and valuation: how to measure a risk, how to value an asset
- Understand how market works, and where they might break down
