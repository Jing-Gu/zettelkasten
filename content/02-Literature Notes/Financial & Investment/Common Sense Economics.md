# Summary & Reflection

## All choices involve costs
Cost is not just measured by money, even something doesn't take money, it is not costless. The time you spend on something will be a cost on doing something else, that is called **opportunity cost**. 
> Everything in life is about opportunity cost. Everyone lives in a world of scarcity and, therefore, must make choices.

## Decisions are made at the margins.
Choices are based on **incremental changes**, not on totals or averages, because **only the next unit is controllable**. Instead of deciding all or nothing, a rational decision compares marginal benefit (benefit of one more unit) and marginal cost (cost of one more unit), and take action once marginal benefit is bigger than marginal cost. Past costs are irrelevant once they are sunk.

**Total framing (wrong):**  
“I’ve already spent $8,000 on this project, I must continue.”

**Marginal framing (correct):**  
“Does spending the _next_ $1 create more than $1 of value?”

Only the next step matters.

This is why sunk costs are cognitive traps.
