# The Book in 3 Sentences

The book has 2 parts to talk: **Wealth** and **Happiness**, which nicely summarizes two critical life subjects.

# Top Quotes
### Understand how wealth is created
Learn to sell. Learn to build. If you can do both, you will be unstoppable.
Arm yourself with specific knowledge, accountability, and leverage. 
Wealth is assets that earn while you sleep.

### Play long-term games with long-term people
When you find the right thing to do, when you find the right people to work with, invest deeply. Sticking with it for decades is really how you make the big returns in your relationships and in your money.

### Find a position of leverage
Each level has increasing leverage, increasing accountability, increasing specific knowledge. You're adding in money-based leverage on top of labor-based leverage. 

### Get paid for your judgement
Judgement, especially demonstrated judgement, with high accountability and a clear track record, is critical.



# Summary & Reflection

Get paid for your judgement
CEO is paid by judgement, but not any work. Someone who demonstrates a slightly better judgement (right 85% of the time instead of 75%) can get millions more pay, because 10% better judgement steering a $100 billion ship is very valuable.

Do smart people win by marginal advantage? Small differences in judgement and capability really get amplified.
